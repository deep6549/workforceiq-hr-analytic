# WorkforceIQ: Employee Attrition & Experience Analytics
An interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, overtime, work-life balance, and employee experience. The dashboard transforms HR data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand employee turnover patterns, identify potential retention concerns, and explore workforce trends through interactive visualizations.

**WorkforceIQ: Employee Attrition & Experience Analytics**
A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard built to explore employee data, analyze attrition patterns, and understand workforce behavior across departments, age groups, job roles, salary ranges, satisfaction levels, and work experience.

The WorkforceIQ dashboard provides an interactive view of workforce composition and employee attrition using structured HR data. It enables users to monitor key HR KPIs, compare attrition across departments, analyze employee satisfaction, and explore relationships between turnover, overtime, work-life balance, salary, and job roles.

The dashboard is designed to support HR analytics, workforce planning, employee retention analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

##Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

**Power BI Desktop** – Main platform used to build the interactive dashboard, reports, and visualizations.

**Power Query** – Used for importing, cleaning, transforming, and preparing HR data for analysis.

**DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create measures for key HR metrics, including total employees, employees who left, and attrition rate.

**Data Modeling** – Used to organize workforce data and support consistent calculations across report visuals.

**Conditional Formatting** – Used in matrix visualizations to highlight differences in attrition rates across job roles and age groups.

**Interactive Slicers** – Used to filter and explore employee data by department and other available workforce attributes.

**Report-Page Tooltips** – Used to display additional HR KPIs and context when users hover over selected visualizations.

**Data Visualization** – KPI cards, bar charts, matrix heatmaps, and other interactive visuals used to communicate workforce insights.

**File Format** – `.pbix` for the Power BI report and `.pbit` where a reusable template is provided.

## Data Source

The dashboard uses structured employee HR data containing workforce and employment-related attributes, including:

- Employee ID and employee demographics
- Age and age groups
- Department and job role
- Monthly income and salary-related information
- Years at the company and work experience
- Employee attrition status
- Job satisfaction and environmental satisfaction
- Work-life balance ratings
- Overtime status
- Other available employee and workplace attributes

The data was prepared and transformed before visualization and analysis in Power BI.

## Features 

###  Business Problem

Organizations collect employee data across departments, job roles, compensation, satisfaction, and work experience. However, raw HR records can make it difficult to identify employee turnover patterns and understand which workforce segments may require further investigation.

Important questions such as:

- What proportion of employees have left the organization?
- Which departments experience higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary across age groups and job roles?
- Is attrition different among employees who work overtime?
- How do job satisfaction and work-life balance relate to employee attrition?
- How do salary levels and years of experience vary across workforce segments?

can be difficult to answer efficiently using raw employee data.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of WorkforceIQ is to convert employee data into an interactive HR analytics solution that allows users to:

- Monitor overall workforce size and attrition metrics.
- Compare attrition rates across departments and employee groups.
- Explore workforce demographics and job-role distribution.
- Analyze employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Investigate attrition patterns associated with overtime.
- Compare salary and work-experience patterns across employee segments.
- Use interactive filters and tooltips to explore HR metrics in greater detail.

### Walkthrough of Key Visuals

 **KPI Cards**

The dashboard displays key human resources indicators, including:

- Total Employees
- Employees Who Left
- Attrition Rate

These KPIs provide an immediate overview of workforce size and employee turnover.

**Department-wise Attrition Analysis**

A comparative chart analyzes attrition across departments, helping users identify departments with relatively higher attrition rates and investigate potential differences in employee retention.

**Workforce Demographics**

Age-group and employee-distribution visuals provide an overview of the workforce composition and help users compare employee segments.

**Job Role Analysis**

Job-role visualizations compare workforce segments and help explore differences in attrition across organizational roles.

**Salary and Work Experience Analysis**

Salary and tenure-related visuals help examine how compensation and years at the company vary across employees and workforce segments.

**Employee Satisfaction Analysis**

Satisfaction-related visuals explore available employee satisfaction measures and their relationship with workforce trends. These patterns can help identify areas that may deserve further investigation.

**Attrition by Overtime**

A dedicated visualization compares attrition rates between employees who work overtime and those who do not. This helps investigate whether overtime status is associated with different attrition patterns.

**Work-Life Balance Analysis**

Work-life balance visuals compare employee groups by their reported balance ratings and help explore how these ratings relate to attrition.

**Attrition Matrix Heatmap**

A matrix comparing job roles and age groups uses conditional formatting to highlight differences in attrition rates. The heatmap makes it easier to spot workforce segments that may warrant closer analysis.

**Interactive Filters**

The dashboard includes interactive slicers and report interactions that allow users to explore the available employee data by department and other supported categories.

**Custom Report-Page Tooltips**

Hovering over selected visuals reveals additional HR metrics, such as total employees, employees who left, and attrition rate. This provides more context without requiring users to navigate away from the main report.

### Business Impact & Insights

**Workforce Monitoring**

KPI cards provide a quick overview of employee headcount and attrition, helping users monitor the overall workforce situation.

 **Department and Job-Role Analysis**

Comparing employee groups helps highlight differences in attrition and identify areas for further HR investigation.

 **Employee Experience Analysis**

Satisfaction and work-life balance measures provide additional context for exploring employee experience and potential retention concerns.

**Overtime Analysis**

Comparing attrition by overtime status helps investigate whether working patterns are associated with differences in employee turnover.

**Compensation and Tenure Analysis**

Salary and years-at-company comparisons help explore how employee retention patterns differ across compensation and experience groups.

**Visual Pattern Recognition**

Conditional formatting and interactive tooltips make it easier to identify noteworthy patterns and access supporting metrics.
**Data-Driven HR Decisions**

The dashboard brings multiple workforce indicators into one interactive report, supporting evidence-based HR discussions and further investigation into employee retention.

*Note: The dashboard highlights associations and patterns in the available data; it does not establish that a particular factor causes employee attrition.*
